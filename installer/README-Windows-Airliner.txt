AIRLINER 1.0 by slapworx
========================

A DJ looper and a vinyl sim in one effect. LOOP grabs a tape loop of the beat the
moment you hit it and drops it when you let go. ON fades in an old record: compression,
surface noise, wow and flutter. ORDER swaps the two.

Windows 10 / 11, 64-bit. VST3 and CLAP.


INSTALLING
----------
1. Close your DAW.
2. Double-click "Install Airliner.exe".
3. If Windows shows a blue "Windows protected your PC" box, click
   "More info", then "Run anyway". (The installer isn't code-signed yet.)
4. Click Next, Install, Finish.
5. Open your DAW and let it find new plug-ins. Most do this on start-up.
   FL Studio: Options > Manage plugins > Find installed plugins.
6. Put Airliner on a track or on the master. It shows up under slapworx.

Rather copy the files yourself? Everything is in the "manual install" folder:
  Airliner.vst3  ->  C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3
  Airliner.clap  ->  C:\Program Files\Common Files\CLAP


WHERE THINGS ARE (after installing)
-----------------------------------
  VST3 plug-in:   C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\Airliner.vst3
  CLAP plug-in:   C:\Program Files\Common Files\CLAP\Airliner.clap
  This file:      C:\Program Files\slapworx\Airliner
  Start menu:     slapworx > Airliner README


GETTING STARTED
---------------
- Drag a knob or fader to change it, double-click to reset.
- MIDI controller: send your controller's MIDI to Airliner (the same way as for any
  MIDI-controlled effect in your DAW), then right-click any control and press the pad
  or turn the knob you want for it. Links are saved on this computer, so the same
  controller works in every project.


UNINSTALLING
------------
Windows Settings > Apps > Airliner > Uninstall, or Start menu > slapworx >
Uninstall Airliner. Your settings and MIDI links are kept.
